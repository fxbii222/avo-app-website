The app

Depending on how you use AVO, we may process:

  • Account data such as phone number or Apple ID sign-in identifiers
  • Profile data you provide: first name, age, photos, interests, relationship goals, self-reported attachment answers, boundaries, and communication preferences
  • Likes, passes, matches, and messages you send
  • Reports, blocks, and moderation records
  • Subscription status from Apple, without your full card number
  • Device and diagnostic data needed to run and secure the app

Until the live AVO backend is connected, data stays on the device. Preview profiles shown in development or App Review are labelled as previews and are not live members.

Why we process data

  • To provide dating, matching, chat, and account features (contract)
  • To keep people 18+, prevent abuse, and handle reports (legitimate interests and legal duty)
  • Waitlist or optional notices, only with consent where required

Sharing

Other users see the public parts of your profile. We use processors such as Apple (App Store, Sign in with Apple, In-App Purchase, push) and, when configured, Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. We do not sell personal data.

Messages and moderation

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. We do not routinely read private chats. Trust and safety staff may access content when investigating a report, a safety risk, suspected underage use, or a terms violation.

Retention and rights

You can request access, correction, export, or deletion at the email above. Account deletion is also offered in the app. We keep data only as long as needed for the service, legal holds, or a documented moderation record.

Children

AVO is not for anyone under 18. We delete accounts that fail age checks.
