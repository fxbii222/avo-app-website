Dating for Avoidants

Finally, Dating That Gets You.

AVO is an international dating app for adults 18 and older who have an avoidant attachment style, or who want to date someone who does. Your space. Your pace. No swipe gestures. Likes and passes are buttons only.

What AVO is

Attachment-aware matching from answers you give yourself. Personal boundaries. A communication pace you choose. A compatibility score that is an explanation, not a diagnosis and not a promise.
